    📖 How to play
    1. Host a game (or Join with a 6-letter invite code). 2 to 8 players can play.
    2. The host picks a scene and the number of rounds, then taps Start.
    3. The first singer picks a starting letter (or one is chosen automatically after 20 seconds).
    4. On your turn, tap Start singing and sing a song that begins with the shown letter. You have 20 seconds to begin and the mic goes live for up to 90 seconds. Tap Finished when done.
    5. Everyone else votes 👍 Valid or 👎 Invalid. A valid song gives +10 points, an invalid one -10.
    6. Then pick the next letter: the next song must start with the first letter of the last word of your song.
    7. Can't think of a song? Tap Pass (running out of time counts as a pass). Everyone else can ✋ raise a hand: the first to raise it sings the same letter for 5 points (👍/👎 vote), then the round carries on.
    8. The game ends when someone reaches 50 points, rounds are over, or only one player is left. Highest score wins! 🏆 A tie goes to a ⚡ Rapid Fire round: 5 random letters, the tied players buzz in, and the most valid answers (then the fastest total response) wins.
    9. Tap a player to 🚩 report them. The host can also ⛔ kick out a player. Use the chat to talk during the game.

    Sign in with Google (on the home screen or in the lobby) to save your history, wins, leaderboard rank and to add friends.
